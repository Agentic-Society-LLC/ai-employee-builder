# Platforms: where the employee lives

Five platforms, two tiers. Researched against official docs on 2026-10-14; anything marked **VERIFY** could not be confirmed from a primary source and should be checked in the product before you rely on it. Products in this space change monthly: when a screen does not match, trust the screen, tell the CEO, and re-derive the steps.

| Platform | Made by | Tier | Who it is for | Runs where | Claude models? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Muse** | Meta | simple | non-technical, consumer and small business, US | Meta's cloud (one computer per Muse) | No (Meta's models) |
| **Grok** (Grok Bot) | xAI, billed through Cursor or SuperGrok | simple | business users, sales / ads / finance / chief-of-staff jobs | xAI's cloud (one computer per account, a screen per Bot) | No (Cursor-managed mix) |
| **Dots** | OpenAI | simple | ChatGPT Pro / Business / Enterprise users | OpenAI's cloud | No (GPT-6 Astra) |
| **Hermes** (Hermes Agent) | Nous Research, open source | advanced | technical or power users, CLI first | your laptop, a VPS, or a sandbox | **Yes** (API key, Claude Max, Opus) |
| **OpenClaw** | OpenClaw Foundation, open source | advanced | technical or power users | your Mac, Linux or Windows box, or a VPS | **Yes** (API key, Claude CLI reuse, Opus) |

## Routing (Phase 5.1 and 5.2)

The rule is Austin's: **tech level 1 to 6, or no intention to wire APIs, MCPs or CLIs → simple tier, Grok by default. Tech level 7 or higher and a real intention to build on APIs, MCPs and advanced workflows → advanced tier, Hermes by default.** Then pick within the tier from what the interview already told you, and name the runner-up:

**Simple tier**
- **Grok** when: the job is a business job (sales, paid media, finance, operations), the CEO may want several Bots as a team, they already pay for SuperGrok, X Premium+ or Cursor, or they said "Grok". The default.
- **Muse** when: the CEO is at tech level 1 to 3, lives in Gmail / Calendar / WhatsApp / iMessage on a Mac, wants free to start, and is in the US. Muse lets you edit `Soul.md`, `Identity.md` and `Memory.md` in the app, so the packet maps straight across.
- **Dots** when: the CEO already pays for ChatGPT Pro, Business Premium or Enterprise and the team lives in ChatGPT, Slack or Teams. Not available on Free / Go / Plus, and not in the EEA, UK or Switzerland at launch.

**Advanced tier**
- **Hermes** when: the CEO wants Claude Opus as the model, many messaging surfaces (Telegram, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal, email, SMS, iMessage via a bridge), real webhooks, cron with cron syntax, and a skills folder. The default.
- **OpenClaw** when: the CEO wants it on their own Mac with native iMessage, the largest skill catalog (ClawHub), the dashboard, or already uses Claude Code / Codex logins it can reuse. Also right when the CEO already runs OpenClaw.
- **Tie-break:** both fit most technical CEOs. Pick Hermes unless one of these is true: they already run OpenClaw, they want native iMessage on a signed-in Mac, or they want a web dashboard over a CLI. Say which of those decided it.

Always state the reason in one line and let the CEO override. Record `platform.choice`, `tier`, `reason`, `runnerUp`, `techLevel` in the tracker. If the choice is a hosted platform, note in the contract that the model is the vendor's and Opus 5.5 is the model for **this build session**, not for the employee's runtime.

## The model for the build

Tell the CEO at the start: this interview and build runs best on **Claude Opus 5.5** because it holds the whole contract, the packet and the platform steps in its head at once. In Claude Code: `/model` → Opus 5.5. In the Claude app: the model picker at the top of the chat. In Cowork: the model menu. If they cannot switch, carry on; say so once and do not mention it again.

## Muse (Meta)

- **What:** Meta's personal AI agent app (iPhone, Android, Mac, web, WhatsApp) that takes actions with your direction. Hosted. Free tier with a weekly usage cap; press reports paid Power ($20/mo) and Maximum ($100/mo) plans: **VERIFY current names and prices in the app.** US-only at launch.
- **Install:** (1) install the iPhone app or open the web or Mac app; (2) sign in with a Meta Account (Facebook, Instagram, email or phone); (3) Muse opens on Chat; type the first request. Docs: ai.meta.com/muse and the Meta Help Center.
- **Where the packet goes:** tap the Assistant icon → Identity / Memory. `Soul.md`, `Identity.md` and `Memory.md` are editable in-app. Paste SOUL.md into Soul.md, IDENTITY.md into Identity.md, and the rendered instructions (packet.md, "Rendering") into Soul.md below the introduction if there is no separate instructions field. Name, avatar, tagline and tone (formal / casual / funny / concise) are set in chat or settings. Skills are Meta-built; you cannot add your own.
- **Routines:** natural language only: "Every Monday at 9am, send me the at-risk client list." Muse confirms; view and change under Assistant → Upcoming. Times use the CEO's time zone. No pause for a routine: cancel or reschedule. DST handling undocumented: **VERIFY by watching the first run after a clock change.**
- **Triggers:** no user-facing webhooks. Some connectors push updates (calendar changes). For "when Stripe gets a new customer", the path is the Zapier connector (Zapier MCP under the hood) in the Small Business connector set: **VERIFY in Settings → Connectors.** Otherwise use a polling routine.
- **Tools:** Settings → Connectors → Connect, or say "Connect my Gmail". Per-connector read-only vs act permissions. Facebook / Instagram / Threads connect through Accounts Center. On a Mac, iMessage, Notes, Reminders, Mail and Calendar can be set Off / Read only / Read and interact per app. Reported business connectors: Zapier, Slack, Stripe, QuickBooks, HighLevel, Klaviyo, Canva, Asana, Dropbox, Figma, Zoom (**VERIFY**). No MCP configuration in the consumer app (**VERIFY**).
- **Talk to it on:** the apps, web, WhatsApp. Not Telegram, Slack or Discord.
- **Stop button:** there is no pause switch. The honest stop is: Settings → Permissions → "Always ask" on every action, then disconnect the connectors the routine uses, then cancel the routine. Write that sequence in the runbook as the stop button and have the CEO rehearse it. Settings → Data Controls → Reset Muse deletes everything (irreversible).
- **Memory:** Memory.md in-app, or ask it to forget. "Download your Muse data" under Data controls.

## Grok (xAI Grok Bot)

- **What:** "your team of always-on agents", beta since Aug 2026. Hosted. No standalone subscription: included with paid Cursor plans, or linked from SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus / Heavy, X Premium+ or a Grok Business seat; usage resets weekly, overage billed through Cursor. Cursor manages model selection: **VERIFY the xAI–Cursor relationship before describing it on a slide.** Docs: docs.x.ai/grok-bot.
- **Install:** (1) install the desktop app (macOS, Windows, Linux) or mobile app; (2) Sign in, finish authentication in the browser; (3) New → Create new Bot, describe its job, grant access as it asks.
- **Where the packet goes:** the Bot's **description** holds the lasting rules: paste the rendered instructions there (SOUL first, then AGENTS, then USER, then the first job). Edit Profile sets the name and avatar. Each recurring job becomes a **skill**: ask the Bot to "save this as a skill" or use Teach a task; invoke with `/`; manage under Connect apps → Your plugins → Manage plugins and skills. One skill per `skills/<job>.md`.
- **Routines:** tell the Bot in plain words ("Every weekday at 7:00 AM, run the morning brief skill and email it to Dana"). It confirms the schedule and time zone and shows the next run. Minimum 5 minutes apart, up to 50 per Bot. Set the Bot's time zone first: Settings → Bot → Timezone. Pause or enable under View conversation details → Routines. Routines may auto-pause if the CEO is away and ignores the keep-running prompt; put that in the runbook. No cron syntax; DST undocumented (**VERIFY after the first clock change**).
- **Triggers:** Cursor account integrations can start a routine from an event (a Slack message, a GitHub notification). Tagging @bot on X hands a post to the Bot. No generic webhook documented (**VERIFY**). For Stripe-style events, poll on a routine.
- **Tools:** Connect apps → Add → sign in in the browser; connectors are account-wide plugins; `@` attaches one to a task. Persistent browser sessions for computer use; a "secure secret request" for keys; the CEO takes over the screen for passwords and 2FA (never the Bot). MCP, Composio and Zapier are not in the official docs (**VERIFY**).
- **Talk to it on:** the desktop and mobile apps, voice, group chats of several Bots, @bot on X. Slack and email appear as drafts the Bot prepares; whether a Bot can *send* an email to its owner on a schedule, or only leave a draft in the owner's mailbox, is not settled by the docs (**VERIFY in-app; until then plan the delivery as a draft the owner opens, or as the Bot's own chat thread**). Call recorders (Fathom, Zoom, Granola) are not on the documented connector list (**VERIFY**); if absent, use the folder fallback in `connections.md`. No official Telegram or iMessage channel (**VERIFY**).
- **Stop button:** no documented stop for a running task (**VERIFY in-app**). Documented: pause the routine, delete the Bot, reset the Bot's computer (last resort). Hiding a Bot does not pause it. Runbook: "pause every routine under Routines; if it is still acting, delete the Bot."
- **Memory:** per-Bot preferences and summaries, corrected in chat; shared files under `/workspace` on the cloud computer. No edit screen.

## Dots (OpenAI)

- **What:** the always-on agent inside ChatGPT, launched Sept 29 2026 on GPT-6 Astra; its own cloud computer and browser. First dot included with ChatGPT Pro, Business Premium and Enterprise (admin-enabled); not on Free, Go or Plus; not in the EEA, UK or Switzerland at launch. Prices: **VERIFY on openai.com/pricing.**
- **Install:** (1) open the ChatGPT desktop app or ChatGPT in a desktop browser (mobile cannot create a dot) and open Your dot; (2) follow the introduction, name and customise it; (3) connect apps and decide whether to connect your computer. The official help article could not be fetched; wording **VERIFY**.
- **Where the packet goes:** no files. Shape it in conversation (paste the rendered instructions as the first message and ask it to adopt them as its standing instructions) and in Settings → Personalization → Custom rules, which set one of four behaviours per action: take action without asking, take action when you say so, ask before taking action, hand off to you. Map AGENTS.md's approval boundary onto those four.
- **Routines:** ask for a fixed schedule in plain words; it appears under Scheduled in the dot's profile (In progress / Scheduled / Completed). By default a dot self-paces its follow-ups; for an employee, insist on fixed schedules. Time zone and DST undocumented (**VERIFY**).
- **Triggers:** reacts to events when the connected service supports them; proactive work between prompts is read-only research. No webhook (**VERIFY**). Poll on a routine for everything else.
- **Tools:** the Plugins tab, the same plugins as ChatGPT and Codex ("4,000+ apps"); optional connection to the CEO's own computer. Whether plugins are MCP under the hood: **VERIFY**.
- **Talk to it on:** ChatGPT desktop, web and mobile, voice calls, Slack, Microsoft Teams (alpha or GA: **VERIFY**); texting "coming soon".
- **Stop button:** Pause stops the current main task; Resume continues. Delegated and scheduled work is stopped under Activity or Scheduled. Deleting the dot is irreversible.
- **Memory:** the dot keeps its own notes, separate from ChatGPT memory; individual memories reportedly cannot be viewed or deleted (**VERIFY**).

## Hermes (Hermes Agent, Nous Research)

- **What:** "the self-improving AI agent", MIT-licensed, free; you pay a model provider. CLI first, with a desktop app for Windows and Apple-silicon Macs. Runs on a laptop, a VPS as a systemd service, or sandboxes (Docker, SSH, Daytona, Modal, Vercel Sandbox). Python 3.14. Docs: hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs · GitHub: NousResearch/hermes-agent.
- **Install:** (1) `curl -fsSL https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/install.sh | bash` (Windows: `iex (irm https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/install.ps1)`); (2) `source ~/.zshrc`; (3) `hermes`, then `hermes model` to pick the provider. Messaging: `hermes gateway setup`.
- **Claude:** `hermes config set model anthropic/claude-opus-5-5` with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, or Claude Max OAuth (Max plan with extra usage credits; Pro cannot), or a setup token.
- **Where the packet goes:** `~/.hermes/SOUL.md` is read verbatim every session as slot one of the system prompt (SOUL.md goes here, introduction first). `AGENTS.md` in the project folder for conventions (AGENTS.md goes here). `~/.hermes/memories/USER.md` (1,375 chars) and `MEMORY.md` (2,200 chars) are snapshotted into every session (a compressed USER.md goes here; the full one stays in the packet). Skills: `~/.hermes/skills/<category>/<job>/SKILL.md` with `name` and `description` frontmatter, optional `references/`, `templates/`, `scripts/`; one per `skills/<job>.md`. Hermes can also import an OpenClaw workspace (`SOUL.md`, `USER.md`, `MEMORY.md`, skills).
- **Routines:** `hermes cron create "weekdays at 7am" "Run the morning-brief skill and deliver it" --name "Morning brief" --deliver telegram`, or in chat `/cron add "every monday 9am" "…"`. Accepts plain words, intervals, cron expressions (`0 7 * * 1-5`) and ISO timestamps. Jobs live in `~/.hermes/cron/jobs.json`, output in `~/.hermes/cron/output/<job>/`. The gateway must be running (60-second tick). Jobs use the machine's local time zone; no time-zone flag documented (**VERIFY `hermes cron create --help`**); DST undocumented, so set the machine's zone to the CEO's and watch the first run after a clock change.
- **Triggers:** a webhook platform in the gateway. In `~/.hermes/.env`: `WEBHOOK_ENABLED=true`, `WEBHOOK_PORT=8644`, `WEBHOOK_SECRET=…`. Then `hermes webhook subscribe stripe-new-customer --events "customer.created" --prompt "New customer: {data.object.email}. Run the onboarding skill, human-first." --deliver telegram --deliver-chat-id <id>` gives `http://host:8644/webhooks/stripe-new-customer` with HMAC verification. `hermes webhook list | test | remove`. Put Hookdeck or similar in front if the box is not reachable from the internet.
- **Tools:** MCP via `mcp_servers:` in `~/.hermes/config.yaml` (stdio `command`/`args`, or `url`/`headers`), `hermes mcp add`, or `hermes mcp install <name>` from a curated catalog. Composio and Zapier are not built in; both ship MCP servers that fit `mcp_servers` (**VERIFY the current server URLs**). Skills can wrap CLIs.
- **Talk to it on:** CLI, Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal, Matrix, Mattermost, email, SMS, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat, LINE, iMessage via BlueBubbles or Photon, Home Assistant.
- **Where secrets go:** `~/.hermes/.env` (API keys, webhook secret) and `~/.hermes/config.yaml` for non-secret settings; never the skills folder, never SOUL.md. The CEO edits the file themselves; you tell them the key name, not the value.
- **Where the controls live:** the cron job's prompt is one line that runs the packet's `runtime/run.mjs` (`go-live.md`, Stage 4); the claim, ceiling, run record and stop button are in that script.
- **Stop button:** one job: `hermes cron pause <job>` / `hermes cron resume <job>`. One channel without a restart: `/platform pause telegram` / `/platform resume telegram`. Everything: `hermes gateway stop`. Current turn: `/stop`. The employee's stop button is `hermes cron pause` for each of its jobs; write the list in the runbook.
- **Memory:** `MEMORY.md` and `USER.md` under `~/.hermes/memories/`, written by the agent's memory tool; set `memory.write_approval: true` so writes queue for `/memory pending | approve | reject`. That is the arbitrate verb, built in. Sessions in `~/.hermes/state.db` with full-text search.

## OpenClaw

- **What:** open-source assistant that runs on your own computer and connects to the chat apps you already use. MIT, developed by the OpenClaw Foundation (formerly Clawdbot and Moltbot). Free; you pay the model provider. Node.js 24.16+. Runs as a LaunchAgent, systemd user unit or Scheduled Task, or on a VPS. Docs: docs.openclaw.ai · GitHub: openclaw/openclaw.
- **Install:** (1) `curl -fsSL https://openclaw.ai/install.sh | bash` (Windows: `iwr -useb https://openclaw.ai/install.ps1 | iex`, or `npx openclaw@latest`); (2) the onboarding wizard (`openclaw onboard --classic` for step by step; needs a Claude Code or Codex login, or an API key); (3) Ctrl-C the foreground Gateway and `openclaw gateway install`. Then `openclaw dashboard` (port 18789).
- **Claude:** provider `anthropic` with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` (`openclaw onboard --auth-choice apiKey`), reuse of the Claude CLI login, or a setup token. Model ref `anthropic/claude-opus-5-5`; set with `openclaw models set anthropic/claude-opus-5-5` or `/model`.
- **Where the packet goes:** the workspace, default `~/.openclaw/workspace` (keep it in a private git repo; never commit secrets; `~/.openclaw/` itself stays out). Loaded every session: `AGENTS.md` (operating instructions; its `## Tools` section is where TOOLS.md's table goes, since the standalone TOOLS.md is no longer read), `SOUL.md`, `USER.md` (4,000-char budget). `IDENTITY.md` is created in the first-run bootstrap ritual (paste the packet's IDENTITY.md over it). `MEMORY.md` is read in the main private session only; `memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md` daily notes; optional `DREAMS.md`. Skills: `<workspace>/skills/<job>/SKILL.md` with `name`/`description` frontmatter (`openclaw skills install @owner/<slug>` from ClawHub). **`HEARTBEAT.md` is legacy and not read**; the packet's `ROUTINES.md` is documentation, and the routines themselves are registered with the command below. Bootstrap files are truncated at 20,000 chars each, 60,000 total: keep the packet short.
- **Routines:** `openclaw automations add --name "Morning brief" --cron "0 7 * * 1-5" --tz "America/Chicago" --message "Run the morning-brief skill and deliver it to Dana." --session isolated` (`openclaw cron` is an alias). Kinds: `--at`, `--every`, `--cron` (5 or 6 fields), `--on-exit`, `--stream-command`. **Use `--tz`**; without it cron uses the host's zone and bare timestamps are UTC. DST transition behaviour undocumented (**VERIFY after the first clock change**). Top-of-hour jobs are staggered up to 5 minutes unless `--exact`. Manage: `enable | disable | edit | run | remove | list | runs`. `run` is the manual trigger for Stage 4. Heartbeat: `agents.defaults.heartbeat.every: "30m"` by default (`"0m"` disables), with `activeHours` and a `timezone`.
- **Triggers:** inbound hooks. Config `hooks.enabled: true`, `hooks.token`, `hooks.path: "/hooks"`. `POST /hooks/agent` with `message`, `deliver` and an `Idempotency-Key` header (that header is the per-event claim); `POST /hooks/wake`; `POST /hooks/<name>` resolved through `hooks.mappings` (match on path or source, optional transform); a `gmail` preset for Pub/Sub. Example: `curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:18789/hooks/agent -H "Authorization: Bearer $HOOK_TOKEN" -H "Idempotency-Key: cus_123" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"message":"New Stripe customer cus_123: run onboarding, human-first.","deliver":false}'`. Also `--trigger-script ./watch.js` on an `every` schedule that fires only when the script returns `fire: true` (a polling trigger in one file).
- **Tools:** MCP client: `openclaw mcp add <name> --url https://… --transport streamable-http --include 'search,read_*'`, or stdio with `--command` / `--arg`; config under `mcp.servers`; `openclaw mcp doctor <name> --probe`. Skills, plugins, `exec`. Composio and Zapier are not built in; use their MCP servers (**VERIFY URLs**). The `--include` allow-list is how you make a connection read-only.
- **Talk to it on:** WebChat (Control UI), Telegram and X bundled; official plugins for iMessage (via `imsg` on a signed-in Mac), Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, Signal, WhatsApp, Google Chat, Matrix, Mattermost, SMS, LINE, IRC.
- **Where secrets go:** outside the workspace, in OpenClaw's own config and credential files under `~/.openclaw/` (the docs keep config, credentials and sessions there and say never to commit them with the workspace); API keys are set through the onboarding wizard or the environment the Gateway service runs with. The exact file for a custom secret such as a Stripe webhook token: **VERIFY in the current docs**; until then use a `.env` the Gateway loads and say so in the runbook.
- **Where the controls live:** the automation's `--message` is one line that runs the packet's `runtime/run.mjs` (`go-live.md`, Stage 4); the claim, ceiling, run record and stop button are in that script, and `--session isolated` keeps each run clean.
- **Stop button:** one job: `openclaw automations disable <jobId>`. Everything, pausable: `openclaw gateway suspend` → `openclaw gateway resume <suspensionId>` (**VERIFY how the id is shown**). Hard stop: `openclaw gateway stop`. All automations: `cron.enabled: false` or `OPENCLAW_SKIP_CRON=1`. Heartbeat off: `every: "0m"`. The employee's stop button is `automations disable` per job; list the ids in the runbook.
- **Memory:** plain Markdown in the workspace; "the model only remembers what gets saved to disk". `memory_search` (hybrid vector plus keyword), `openclaw memory status | search | index`, a pre-compaction memory flush, and a nightly **dreaming** pass that promotes daily notes into `MEMORY.md` (the synthesize verb, built in; the CEO still arbitrates by editing the file).
